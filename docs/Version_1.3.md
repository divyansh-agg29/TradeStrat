# Trading Strategy Analysis Platform - Version 1.3 Summary

## Overview

Version 1.3 focuses on enhancing the platform's analytical capabilities by introducing benchmark comparison and expanding the available risk metrics. This release allows users to compare the performance of an active trading strategy against a passive Buy & Hold investment, providing valuable context when evaluating backtest results.

In addition, the Analytics Engine has been extended with two widely used risk-adjusted performance metrics—Sortino Ratio and Calmar Ratio—offering a more comprehensive assessment of strategy performance.

---

## Buy & Hold Benchmark

Version 1.3 introduces a Buy & Hold benchmark that serves as a passive investment baseline for every backtest.

Alongside the selected trading strategy, the platform now computes the hypothetical portfolio value that would have resulted from investing the entire initial capital in the selected stock on the first trading day and holding it until the end of the backtest period.

This enables users to answer one of the most important questions in strategy evaluation:

> **"Did the trading strategy actually outperform simply buying and holding the stock?"**

The benchmark calculation is performed automatically during the backtesting workflow and integrates seamlessly with the existing analytics pipeline without affecting the modular architecture of the application.

---

## Benchmark Metrics

To support strategy comparison, a new Benchmark Metrics section has been added to the dashboard.

The following metrics are now available:

- **Buy & Hold Final Value** – Final portfolio value of the passive investment.
- **Buy & Hold Return** – Overall percentage return generated by the benchmark.
- **Strategy Alpha** – Difference between the strategy return and the Buy & Hold return, indicating whether the strategy outperformed or underperformed the benchmark.

These metrics provide an immediate summary of how the active trading strategy compares to a simple long-term investment approach.

---

## Extended Risk Metrics

The Analytics Engine has been expanded with two additional risk-adjusted performance metrics.

### Sortino Ratio

The Sortino Ratio measures risk-adjusted returns while considering only downside volatility, making it particularly useful for evaluating strategies that aim to minimize negative returns rather than overall price fluctuations.

### Calmar Ratio

The Calmar Ratio evaluates performance by comparing the strategy's annualized return (CAGR) against its maximum drawdown, providing insight into how efficiently returns were generated relative to the largest observed loss.

These additions complement the existing Volatility, Sharpe Ratio, and Maximum Drawdown metrics, providing a more complete assessment of portfolio risk and performance.

---

## Frontend Enhancements

The frontend dashboard has been updated to present benchmark comparison results in a clear and intuitive manner.

Enhancements include:

- A dedicated **Benchmark Metrics** section displaying Buy & Hold performance alongside strategy performance.
- Buy & Hold equity curve overlaid on the existing Equity Curve chart for direct visual comparison.
- Updated Risk Metrics section including the newly added Sortino Ratio and Calmar Ratio.

These improvements allow users to evaluate both numerical performance and portfolio behavior throughout the entire backtest period.

---

## Summary

Version 1.3 strengthens the platform's analytical capabilities by introducing meaningful benchmark comparison and expanding the available risk metrics. By allowing users to compare active trading strategies against a passive Buy & Hold investment while providing additional measures of risk-adjusted performance, the platform delivers a more comprehensive and informative backtesting experience without requiring major architectural changes.
